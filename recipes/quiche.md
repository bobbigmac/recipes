# Simple Quiche

## Ingredients
- 1 sheet shortcrust pastry
- 4 eggs
- 200ml cream or milk
- 100g grated cheese
- Salt and pepper
- Optional cooked bacon or onion

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 180°C. Line a 20cm tin with pastry, prick the base, and blind bake for 10 min.
2. Whisk eggs, cream, cheese, salt, pepper, and any filling.
3. Pour into the case and bake for 25-30 min, until set and golden.
